About Our Diabetes Prediction Tool

This tool uses machine learning to assess your risk of diabetes based on various health parameters.

Parameters Used:

  • Number of Pregnancies
  • Glucose Level
  • Blood Pressure
  • Skin Thickness
  • Insulin Level
  • BMI (Body Mass Index)
  • Diabetes Pedigree Function
  • Age

Note: This tool is for educational purposes only. Please consult with a healthcare professional for medical advice.
